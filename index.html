About Us

Ogre Head Studio (OHS) is an independent video game development studio based in Hyderabad, India, founded in 2014 by Zainuddeen 'Zain' Fahadh.

We create original video games inspired by India's diverse folklore, mythology, culture, and traditions. Our goal is to bring unique Indian stories and worlds to a global audience through engaging gameplay, distinctive art, and thoughtful game design.

In addition to developing our own games, we provide professional game development services to studios and companies around the world, including game art, design, development, and co-development.

Ogre Head Studio is a bootstrapped and independently operated game development company with no external funding. We are a creative team passionate about building original games from India for players around the world.

Our Services

GAME ART

Our artists create high-quality 2D and 3D game art across a range of visual styles, from stylized to realistic, tailored to your project's artistic direction. From concept art and illustrations to 3D character models, environments, and props, we deliver art assets that bring your game world to life.

GAME DESIGN

"Gameplay is king!" is our motto. We bring extensive experience in designing engaging gameplay systems, immersive game worlds, compelling narratives, intuitive UI/UX, and balanced game economies, tailored to the unique needs of your project.

GAME DEV

We develop games using industry-leading engines such as Unity and Unreal Engine. Our developers bring strong programming expertise in C, C++, and C#, delivering robust gameplay systems and technical solutions tailored to your project's requirements.

TECH ART

Our tech team brings together engineering expertise and artistic sensibility. We create captivating visual effects, develop optimized shaders, and build custom tools and pipelines that streamline development and bring creative visions to life.

CO-DEV

Missing a skill set or an extra pair of hands on your team? We've got you covered. Whether you need specialised talent or additional development support, we can help bring your game to life. Get in touch with us for a quote, and let's build something epic together!

END-TO-END DEV

Have a game idea you'd love to bring to life? We're a one-stop shop for game development, offering everything you need to turn your vision into a playable reality. Let's build something epic together!

FOR INDIES & AAA

Whether you're a solo developer or a large game studio, if you're passionate about your project, we'd love to be part of your journey and help bring it to life.

CONSULTATION

Stuck in production or need an extra hand bringing your game to life? We're here to help. Let's get your project moving!
