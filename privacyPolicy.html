1. Introduction

Welcome to Ogre Head Studio ("we", "our", or "us").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information submitted through our website.

2. Information We Collect

Our website has a simple contact form that collects only the following information:

  • Name: To know who is contacting us.
  • Email Address: To respond to your inquiry
  • Description: To understand your message, question, or projection inquiry

We do not require you to provide any additional personal information through our contact form.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information submitted through our contact form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and messages.
  • Communicate with you regarding potential projects, collaborations, or business opportunities.
  • Provide information or assistance related to your request.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or use your personal information for advertising or unrelated marketing purposes.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses Google Analytics to collect information about website usage and visitor interactions. Google Analytics uses cookies to help us understand how visitors use our website and improve its content and functionality.

Analytics cookies are activated only after visitors explicitly provide their consent through our cookie consent banner. If a visitor declines consent, Google Analytics cookies are not activated.

The information collected may include pages visited, time spent on the website, browser type, device information, and general geographic location.

We use this information solely for website analytics and performance measurement. We do not use cookies for advertising, behavioural profiling, or targeted advertising.

Google Analytics is a third-party service provided by Google. Information collected through this service may be processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for commercial or advertising purposes.

Your information may be accessible to our authorised personnel solely for the purpose of responding to your enquiry and managing communications.

We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law or a valid legal request.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information submitted through our contact form against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

We retain submitted information only for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry, maintain relevant business communications, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request access to the personal information you have provided.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal obligations.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the details below.

WOur website may contain links to external websites or services. Ogre Head Studio is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external websites. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, business practices, or applicable legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
